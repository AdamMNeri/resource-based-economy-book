# Chapter 1: The Case for Change: Structural Failures of the Monetary Paradigm

## Core Narrative
Demonstrate that humanity's ongoing crises (inequality, ecological collapse, geopolitical war) are not accidental oversights but mathematical consequences of debt-based fiat money.

## Key Concepts & Sources
- **Bank money creation**: 97% of money created as private bank debt with interest [379, 387].
- **The exponential growth compound interest trap** on a finite planet [380, 388].
- **Empirical proof linking top 1% wealth concentration to financial crises** and asset bubbles (Jakurti 2025) [679, 690, 691].
- **Market failure and unpriced negative externalities** ($5.1T air pollution, $10.6T land degradation, climate crisis) [1059, 1060, 1062, 1064].
- **Wasteful competition**: $2.44T in global military spending [216], $62B healthcare marketing waste [26], and grant proposal waste [23].
