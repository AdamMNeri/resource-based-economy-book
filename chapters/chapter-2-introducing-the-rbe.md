# Chapter 2: Introducing the Resource-Based Economy ("Resourceism")

## Core Narrative
Define RBE not as a political ideology, but as a scientific methodology for planetary management and human flourishing [4, 1042].

## Key Concepts & Sources
- **Definition of RBE / Resourceism**: Earth's resources declared the common heritage of all humanity [4, 5, 1042, 1052].
- **Contrast between rationing via artificial monetary scarcity vs distribution based on physical resource abundance** [270, 1044, 1052].
- **Re-evaluating property rights**: shifting from private ownership of means of production to universal unencumbered access [268, 281, 1045, 1053].
- **Eliminating planned obsolescence, overproduction, and predatory advertising** [270, 364, 1054].
