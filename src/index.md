# Beyond Money: A Systems Architecture for Human Flourishing and Planetary Stewardship

**Author:** Adam Neri  
**Project Lead & Concept:** Adam Neri  
**Last Revised:** 2026-08-12 13:01:42 EDT

## Project Goal
This project aims to develop an open-source book that presents a persuasive, empirically grounded, and visionary blueprint for transitioning humanity away from a debt-based monetary system to a Resource-Based Economy (RBE). This transition will be facilitated by the United Nations system, where planetary resources are declared the common heritage of all people.

## Core Narrative Objective
The book must be written to be not merely understood, but deeply desired. It must dismantle legacy assumptions about money, market efficiency, and human motivation, while offering a pragmatic, scalable, and technologically feasible roadmap for global transition.
